- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine

- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** they are likely conducting music discovery outside of the tool just to HOPEFULLY bring their discoveries into StreamLine
